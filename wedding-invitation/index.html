Chapter One to Forever

OUR LOVE STORY

Two hearts, one faith, a love guided by grace.

Chapter 01

The First Meeting

Some moments seem ordinary until we look back and realise God was writing something extraordinary. I initially had plans to attend another institution and pursue a different career path, but through divine intervention, my journey took a different turn. It was there, in the school fellowship, that we first met. We served in the same department, where she was the Head of Department. A year after her graduation, our paths crossed again, and what began as an ordinary encounter became the beginning of a beautiful love story neither of us could have imagined..

Chapter 02

The Beginning

Jeremiah 1:12 Then said the Lord unto me, Thou hast well seen: for I will hasten my word to perform it. What began as a beautiful connection gradually blossomed into a journey of faith and love. God confirmed His word and perfected His promises in our lives. Through prayers, deeper seeking and divine guidance, our love grew stronger. By His grace, we found the courage to press on, trusting Him every step of the way..

Chapter 03

Growing in Love and Faith

Habakkuk 2:3 – For the vision is yet for an appointed time... though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come. As we journeyed together, our love grew deeper, strengthened by faith, prayers and God's unfailing promises. We learned to encourage, support and stand by each other, trusting God's timing and holding firmly to His Word. Through every season, we became more convinced that what God had begun, He was faithful to complete.

Chapter 04

A Covenant of Forever

With grateful hearts, we have chosen to walk together in faith, love and purpose. As we join our lives before God, we look forward to building a home rooted in Christ, guided by His Word and filled with grace. Our forever begins here.
